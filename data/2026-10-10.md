We benchmark TPSC+ against DiagMC results for the Matsubara spectral proxy $-\mathrm{Im}[\mathcal{G}(\mathbf{k},iπT)]/π$. TPSC+ underestimates pseudogap suppression in the strong-interaction regime, reproducing DiagMC behavior at lower temperatures or doping. This proxy is equivalent to the spectral function with thermal broadening $η=πT$, which obscures features when $πT$ is not the smallest energy scale. Using $A(\mathbf{k},0)$, we find that hole-like Fermi surfaces emerge at any interaction strength at low temperature, even at weak coupling.
